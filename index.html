How this works

Listening

Tap the mic to start. This uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, so your voice is sent to your browser's own recognition service (for example Google's on Chrome, Apple's on Safari) to be converted to text. It's not sent anywhere by this app itself, and this page has no server of its own. It needs an internet connection and a page loaded over https:// (or localhost) to work, plain http will not get microphone access.

Autosave

While you're listening, the transcript is saved automatically to this browser's local storage every couple of seconds. That storage lives only on this device, in this browser. It is not synced to the cloud, to OneDrive, or to any other device automatically. Saved sessions appear in the "Saved on this device" list below the transcript, where you can reload or delete them later.

Summarize

Pulls out the handful of sentences that repeat the most information-dense words in the transcript. It runs entirely on your device, with no AI and no internet call involved. It's a rough skim, useful as a starting point, not a substitute for reading the full transcript.

Copy, Download, Share

Copy puts the transcript on your clipboard. Download saves it as a .txt file to your device's downloads. Share (where your browser supports it) opens your phone's normal share sheet, so you can send the transcript straight to an app like OneDrive, Mail, or Messages in one tap.

Privacy

Nothing typed or spoken here is uploaded to any server run by this app. Saved transcripts stay on the device and in the browser where they were recorded, until you choose to copy, download, or share them elsewhere.
